# Bruel Tchiofouo Yemelo

**Development economist — geospatial data science, applied econometrics and machine learning for climate and agriculture.**

I work in Python on geolocated data and climate rasters. My Master's dissertation geocoded 39,585 African firms and matched them to high-resolution climate series to estimate how climate anomalies shape investment, and my current code reads Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, ERA5 and SRTM together over African farmland. Alongside that, I work on impact evaluation and on measurement from household surveys.

## Background

Master 2 in Development Economics, Economic Analysis track (AED), and Master 2 in Development Economics, Project Analysis track (APD), both taken inside the selective Magistère at CERDI, Université Clermont Auvergne. Maîtrise, a four-year degree, in Econometrics at the University of Douala. Research assistant at CIRAD (UMR Innovation, Montpellier) and at the Università di Trento, and economist-statistician at the Ministry of the Economy, Planning and Regional Development in Cameroon.

## What I work with

**Python, geospatial** — NumPy, pandas, geopandas, shapely, fiona, xarray, rasterio, rioxarray and rasterstats, which together are how I use GDAL, with scikit-learn on top. Coordinate reference systems and reprojection, buffers, spatial joins, zonal statistics, raster masking. QGIS.

**Earth observation** — Sentinel-1 backscatter, Sentinel-2 cloud masking on the scene classification and QA60 bands, NDVI, NDWI, NDMI and NBR, median composites, phenology statistics, ERA5 reanalysis, SRTM terrain, STAC catalogue search, Earth Engine Python API.

**Causal inference** — randomised trials, difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity, propensity score matching, instrumental variables, with balance tests and placebos.

**Machine learning** — regularised regression, random forests, gradient boosting, pipelines, spatial and time-series cross-validation, conformal prediction, SHAP interpretation.

**Survey data** — sampling design, weighting, non-response correction, harmonisation across waves, Rasch scaling.

**Other tools** — Stata, R, SQL, Git, LaTeX.
