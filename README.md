# Bruel Tchiofouo Yemelo

**Applied Economist & Data Scientist. Causal inference, applied machine learning, AI economics research.**

I work at the intersection of econometrics and machine learning: impact evaluation, panel data analysis, and predictive modeling applied to development economics, agriculture, and public policy. Alongside this, I run independent research on the economics and governance of AI (see my [portfolio](https://tchiofoubruel-prog.github.io/Portfolio-Data-Analyst-/) for the current project list).

## Background

M.Sc. Economic Analysis and Policy and M.Sc. Project Management, CERDI (Université Clermont Auvergne). B.Sc. Applied Economics, Université de Douala. Experience spans CIRAD/CGIAR agricultural research, the University of Trento, and YouthShare Initiatives, plus the independent research below.

## What I work with

**Econometric methods**
Randomized controlled trials, difference-in-differences, propensity score matching, regression discontinuity design, instrumental variables, panel fixed and random effects, dynamic panel models, time series analysis.

**Machine learning**
Random Forest, XGBoost, SARIMAX, SHAP for interpretability, scikit-learn, TensorFlow.

**Software**
Stata (advanced), R (advanced), Python (advanced), EViews, MATLAB.

**Geospatial and remote sensing**
QGIS, ArcGIS, Google Earth Engine, geopandas, rasterstats.

**Data collection**
KoBoToolbox, SurveyCTO, ODK.
