# Bruel Tchiofouo Yemelo

**Quantitative Economist & Data Scientist — impact evaluation, applied machine learning, AI economics research.**

I work at the intersection of econometrics and machine learning: impact evaluation, panel data analysis, and predictive modeling applied to development economics, agriculture, and public policy. Alongside this, I run independent research on the economics and governance of AI in developing economies (see my [portfolio](https://tchiofoubruel-prog.github.io/Portfolio-Data-Analyst-/) for the current project list).

## Background

M.Sc. Economic Analysis and Policy and M.Sc. Project Management, CERDI (Université Clermont Auvergne). B.Sc. Applied Economics, Université de Douala. Experience spans CIRAD/CGIAR agricultural research economics, applied econometrics at the Università di Trento, and international project leadership at YouthShare Initiatives.

## What I work with

**Econometric methods** — RCT, difference-in-differences, propensity score matching, regression discontinuity, instrumental variables, panel fixed/random effects, dynamic panels, time series (ARIMA/SARIMAX).

**Machine learning** — Random Forest, XGBoost, SHAP interpretability, scikit-learn.

**Software** — R, Stata, Python, SQL, EViews · Tableau, Power BI · QGIS, ArcGIS, geopandas, rasterstats · SurveyCTO, KoBoToolbox, ODK.
